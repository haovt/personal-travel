## Context (verify before you rely on it)

- Part of TP. Hồ Chí Minh. Check its current administrative name after the 2025 reform.
- The Cần Giờ Mangrove Forest is a UNESCO **Biosphere Reserve** (verify the year). That is a different status from World Heritage, so don't mix them up.
- A huge, flat area of mangroves and rivers: the extract can be **large** (many rivers and water polygons). Keep the bbox to the visitor areas.

## Location ideas (starting points only, not facts)

Rừng ngập mặn Cần Giờ; khu du lịch Vàm Sát (đài quan sát, rừng chim); đảo Khỉ (Lâm viên Cần Giờ); địa đạo Rừng Sác (history); biển Cần Thạnh / bãi biển 30/4; chợ Hàng Dương (hải sản). Food: ba khía, sò, hải sản at chợ Hàng Dương: verify which dishes are typical before claiming it.

Pick 5–8 locations with at least 2 food locations. Every coordinate and fact must come from a
source you read (OSM / Wikidata / Wikipedia). Location ids must be `can-gio-<slug>`.

## Photos

 Food photos may be scarce: ask early.

Photo count (owner, updated 2026-11-08): **at most 3 photos per place**, both the destination and
each location (food too). Aim for exactly 3; fewer only if no suitable licensed photo exists.

Wikimedia Commons first (CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA). If a minimum can't be met, use Flickr (CC, never ND), Unsplash or Pexels as described in the playbook (step B4b). Report gaps instead of padding.

## Map notes

Consider two zones: the Cần Thạnh coast (dense) and Vàm Sát (forest). A far place gets an off-screen hint pill, so the bbox needn't cover everything at full detail.
