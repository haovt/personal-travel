## Context (verify before you rely on it)

- Coastal city. After the 2025 reform Bà Rịa – Vũng Tàu is reported as merged into **TP. Hồ Chí Minh** (verify the exact status and how Vietnamese sources describe it now).
- Peninsula between Bãi Trước and Bãi Sau, with the hills Núi Nhỏ and Núi Lớn.

## Location ideas (starting points only, not facts)

Tượng Chúa Kitô Vua (núi Nhỏ; modern work → pre-2023 photos); ngọn hải đăng Vũng Tàu; Bãi Sau (Thùy Vân); Bãi Trước; mũi Nghinh Phong; Hòn Bà; Bạch Dinh; đồi Con Heo. Food: bánh khọt, lẩu cá đuối, hải sản at a market (e.g. chợ Xóm Lưới).

Pick 5–8 locations with at least 2 food locations. Every coordinate and fact must come from a
source you read (OSM / Wikidata / Wikipedia). Location ids must be `vung-tau-<slug>`.

## Photos

The beaches can have up to 10 photos each. Food (bánh khọt) needs ≥ 3 photos.

Minimums (owner):
- ≥ 5 destination photos;
- ≥ 3 per location;
- ≥ 3 per food location;
- up to 10 for food, beach and mountain/forest places.

Wikimedia Commons first (CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA). If a minimum can't be met, use Flickr (CC, never ND), Unsplash or Pexels as described in the playbook (step B4b). Report gaps instead of padding.
