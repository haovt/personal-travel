## Context (verify before you rely on it)

- Coastal city in Khánh Hòa. Khánh Hòa was merged with a neighbour in 2025 (verify).
- Coastal with offshore islands (Hòn Tre, Hòn Mun…): the coastline closes into land, and the islands are closed rings.

## Location ideas (starting points only, not facts)

Tháp Bà Ponagar (Cham towers → history); Hòn Chồng; nhà thờ Núi (Chánh tòa Kitô Vua); chùa Long Sơn; Viện Hải dương học; bãi biển Trần Phú; đảo Hòn Mun (lặn biển → experience); chợ Đầm. Food: bún chả cá Nha Trang, nem nướng Ninh Hòa, bánh căn.

Pick 5–8 locations with at least 2 food locations. Every coordinate and fact must come from a
source you read (OSM / Wikidata / Wikipedia). Location ids must be `nha-trang-<slug>`.

## Photos

Commons coverage is good for Ponagar, the beach and the cathedral. Modern resort/cable-car structures: pre-2023 uploads only.

Photo count (owner, updated 2026-11-08): **at most 3 photos per place**, both the destination and
each location (food too). Aim for exactly 3; fewer only if no suitable licensed photo exists.

Wikimedia Commons first (CC0 / PD / CC BY / CC BY-SA). If Commons can't supply 3, use Flickr (CC, never ND), Unsplash or Pexels as described in the playbook (step B4b). Report gaps instead of padding.
